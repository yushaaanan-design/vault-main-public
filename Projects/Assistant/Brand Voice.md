# Brand Voice (clipping channel, Jynxzi source) - DRAFT v0 by Claude, 2026-10-23, for you to edit

The Voice Check reads this note on every run (first 4,000 characters), so edit it freely: keep one rule per bullet. Everything here comes from the clipping vault's Jynxzi playbook and Format Catalog, not from guesses; where it is my reading it is marked (inference).

## Voice
- Fast, hyped, a little funny, like a friend sending you the clip. Short words, no corporate talk.
- The label states the premise in plain words ("He gets outplayed by a random", not an in-joke only fans understand). Descriptive labels travel better than insider labels (playbook: the 4.7M compilation vs the 32k one).
- Curiosity titles are fine, but the clip must pay off the title in its own length. A title that promises something the clip does not show is a rule break.
- One idea per clip: setup, tension, payoff. The point is in the first second.
- Quote a line only if it is in the clip, word for word. Speech recognition errors mean we do not quote when unsure.

## Never
- No slurs, hate, sexual violence, self-harm, or targeting a private person. If a clip needs that to work, skip the clip.
- No mocking real people for who they are. Banter between streamers is fine only when the clip shows it.
- No claims about money, health or real-world events that the clip does not show.
- No body-focused or flirty framing: that content sits at the bottom of the channel (124k-180k vs 38M for the top hooks).
- No fake urgency ("you won't believe", "gone wrong") unless the clip delivers it.
- No hashtags inside the label or the title.

## Limits (also checked by code in `config/voice.json`)
- Label: 8 words or fewer. Title: 80 characters or fewer.
- Blocked topic words in the config list force a flag (review by you, never auto-used).

## Hooks that worked in the study (see Format Catalog, Hook Measurements)
- In-media-res shout (the first second is the key line), brag/claim, on-screen claim, title-card claim, to-camera challenge, question opening.
- Collabs and reactions to better players pull the most; the community's bond with him is personal (playbook, user insight).

## Open for you
- Words or running jokes we may use or must avoid.
- Whether the channel is "Jynxzi clips" fan-style or a neutral clip brand (affects labels and names). (inference: neutral label, the brand name is your decision)
