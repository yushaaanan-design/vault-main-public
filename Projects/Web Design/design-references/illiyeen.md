# Reference — ILLIYEEN (ilyn.global)

Bangladeshi multi-brand lifestyle retailer. Exhaustive structural crawl — every top-level nav category and its subcategories, filter patterns, and policy-page structure. Reference for: product photography treatment (clean, arch-frame studio shots), inline size-guide pattern, category breadth, tiering system.

## Top utility bar
Support email link, contact link, store locations link, apps link — sits above the main nav, small text.

## Main navigation (top-level)
Final Sale · Beat the Heat · Summer Edition · Men · Women · Kids · Footwear · Fragrance · Accessories · Furniture · Gift Card · Inside Story (blog)

Every clothing/accessory category uses a recurring **tier system** across almost all product types: Premium, Luxury, Platinum, Sahara, and occasionally Ambassador/Legends/Max/Executive/W&S (their in-house sub-brand). This tiering is the site's core merchandising pattern — it's how a single product type (e.g. Panjabi) is split into price/quality bands rather than having separate product lines.

### Shipping Policy page structure (representative of their policy-page pattern)
1. Overview (nationwide + worldwide shipping intro)
2. Delivery timeline table — by zone (Inside Dhaka / Outside Dhaka / Worldwide): order time, delivery type, cost, estimated date
3. Footnotes (e.g. "Free for orders above BDT 5000/-" inside Dhaka; no urgent delivery on certain days; international cost varies)
4. "Closed Box Delivery" policy — products can't be returned to delivery personnel on the spot, video proof required for issues
5. FAQ section (tracking, non-arrival, receiving on someone's behalf, delayed/incorrect orders)
6. Separate furniture-specific shipping subsection (21–28 working day lead time, free installation)
7. Contact info block

**Category-page template — confirmed identical across Men/Women/Kids/Footwear/Fragrance/Accessories, ONE exception:**
Every category (Women, Kids, Footwear, Fragrance, Accessories) uses the exact same layout: full-width cinematic lifestyle photo (unique art direction per category — a marble/gold arch scene for Women, a dark arch/spotlight scene for Kids, a stone-steps scene for Footwear, a silk/perfume-bottle scene for Fragrance, a windowsill/handbag scene for Accessories) → "[Category]: Shop By Category" heading → a horizontal scroll row of circular subcategory tiles with a product photo inside each circle.
- **Furniture breaks this template entirely**: after its own hero photo, it goes to "Shop By Room" (not "Shop By Category") and switches to large rectangular room-scene banners with overlaid heading + description + a "Shop Now" button per room — reflecting the structural crawl's earlier finding that Furniture is organized by room, not tier/type. Visual template genuinely adapts to the category's own logic, not a one-size-fits-all component.

**Product page variant — size selector adapts to product type:**
- Apparel (Panjabi, Dress Set): XS–3XL / XS–2XL letter sizes as pill buttons
- Footwear (Sandals): **EU shoe sizes 40–45** as pill buttons — same component, numeric values swapped in, confirming (like Maneel Club's jeans finding) that size options are data-driven per product, not hardcoded to one system
- CTA buttons also vary by stock/product: the Women's Dress Set (BDT 14,950) shows both "Add to bag" AND "Find in store"; the Footwear sandals show **only** "Find in store" (no working "Add to bag") — likely an online-stock-availability state, worth designing for in Fable ("find in store" as a fallback CTA when a variant is unavailable online, not just a binary add-to-cart/sold-out).

**Cart is a slide-over panel, not a separate page** — clicking the bag icon opens an in-page panel rather than navigating to `/cart`.

**Colors:**
- Body background: `#F5F5F7` (very light warm grey, not pure white)
- Body text: `#000000`
- Hero promo banner: deep maroon/oxblood (`~#7A1F1F`) textured background, diagonally split with a cream/beige band (`~#E8E0D0`), bold white condensed display text ("FINAL SALE / LAST CHAPTER / 60%+ OFF")
- Discount/sale price text: crimson red (`~#C4282F`)
- Primary CTA button ("Add to bag"): solid black background, white text, small lock icon
- Product photo backdrop: near-white/light grey studio background with a tan/wood arch frame around the garment (their signature product-photo device)

**Typography:**
- Font family: `GeneralSans, sans-serif` (a clean geometric grotesque, medium weight ~500 for headings)
- Heading sizes seen: 24px / 30px / 48px, weight 500 — no heavy/bold headline weight, relies on size for hierarchy rather than boldness
- Body/nav text: small, quiet, plenty of letter-spacing on the promo banner's condensed display type

**Layout:**
- Desktop: 2-column hero grid (one large promo tile + two stacked smaller tiles) — asymmetric, not a single full-width hero
- Nav: single-row, all top-level categories visible at once (horizontal scroll/overflow arrow past "Gift" on smaller desktop widths), utility bar above it (delivery region, contact, store locator)
- Mobile: nav collapses to hamburger + centered logo + search; **bottom tab bar** (Home / Store Locations / Shopping Bag / Account) — an app-like persistent nav pattern, not just a header
- Product page: classic 50/50 split — large image left (with arch-frame), info column right, generous white space, no clutter

## Takeaways for Fable
- **The tier system (Premium/Luxury/Platinum/etc.) is Illiyeen's core scaling mechanism** — not relevant at Fable's 2-product launch, but this is the pattern to adopt later if/when the catalog grows past a handful of items, rather than inventing a new one.
- Filter bar pattern (**Color / Size / Price / Sort**) is simple and directly reusable once Fable has more than ~5-10 products; skip it for launch, it's dead weight on a 2-product catalog.
- Inline expandable size guide + explicit fabric composition/care block is a strong trust-building pattern for a new/unknown brand — worth adopting on Fable's product page regardless of catalog size.
- Discount-badge + strikethrough-price pattern is universal across all 3 references (Illiyeen, Get The Juice, Maneel Club) — treat as a near-mandatory component for Fable's product card.
- Shipping-policy page structure (timeline table + FAQ + closed-box/proof-of-damage policy) is a good template for Fable's own shipping/exchange policy page, especially the closed-box/video-proof clause given COD is in scope.
- **Size selector must be data-driven, not hardcoded** — confirmed again here (letter sizes for apparel, EU numeric sizes for footwear, same UI component). Reinforces the same finding from Maneel Club's jeans page — treat this as settled for Fable's Prisma `Variant` schema, not an open question.
- **Cart-as-slide-over-panel** (not a dedicated page) is worth considering for Fable — keeps the customer in context instead of a full navigation away, especially useful on a small catalog where the cart is usually 1-2 items.
- A **"Find in store" fallback CTA** for unavailable-online variants is a real pattern worth having in reserve, though not needed for Fable's 2-product launch (no physical store yet).
- Category hero art direction is bespoke per category, not a generic template — confirms visual effort should scale with how many categories Fable actually has (irrelevant at 2 products now, relevant later when the catalog grows toward Illiyeen's scale).

## Scroll behavior (re-scrape 2026-09-26)
Screenshots: `screenshots/illiyeen-scroll-{0,10,25,35,42,50,75,100}-desktop.png`, `illiyeen-scroll-{0,50,100}-mobile.png`.
- **Stack**: Tailwind + **Swiper** (6 `.swiper` instances, inline `translate3d(...) rotateX() rotateY()` on slides = coverflow effect). Not Shopify. No GSAP/ScrollTrigger/AOS/`[data-scroll]`/pin-spacers — **no scroll-triggered reveal animations**.
- **What animates**: 27 `animate-*` elements are all the **pulsing hotspot dots** (CSS keyframes `pingg`, Tailwind `animate-ping`-style) on the shop-the-look panel; a `blink` keyframe; Swiper rails/coverflow on drag/arrow.
- **Header behavior (the one scroll-driven effect)**: nav starts `relative` under the utility bar, then switches to `fixed` after you scroll past it (`transition-all duration-500`), rendered as a **translucent frosted/gradient bar** over content. Utility bar scrolls away.
- **Lazy loading**: document height grows from 11,112 → 11,760px while scrolling (sections/images load on approach).
- **Mobile**: hamburger + centered logo, fixed bottom tab bar (`lg:hidden fixed bottom-0`), same rails become swipeable; still no reveal animation.
- Takeaway: the "premium" feel is art direction + dark marble/editorial sections + a 3D coverflow + hotspots, not scroll choreography.
